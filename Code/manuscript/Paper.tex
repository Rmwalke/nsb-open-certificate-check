\begin{abstract}
We consider the unforced three-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes
equations on the normalized $2\pi$-periodic torus at viscosity one.
For $W=(\sin z,\sin x,\sin y)$ and every smooth solenoidal, real,
mean-zero perturbation satisfying
$\sum_{k\ne0}|k|^{-1}|\widehat h(k)|\le10^{-4}$, we certify convergence
of the ordinary Picard sequence starting from the complete heat flow
$e^{t\Delta}(W+h)$. Convergence holds on the whole half-line in
$\|f\|_{\mathcal X}=(\int_0^\infty(\sum_{k\ne0}|\widehat f(k)|)^2\,dt)^{1/2}$, with error at iteration $n\ge3$
at most $6(9/10)^{n-3}$ and $\int_0^\infty A_0(u(t))^2\,dt<9$,
where $A_0(f)=\sum_{k\ne0}|\widehat f(k)|$.
The proof retains the full causal history in a finite block norm and verifies
entry of the actual heat-seeded third iterate into one invariant set.
Separate critical-Fourier comparison estimates establish larger explicit
global-regularity neighborhoods. A validated finite-reference certificate
also gives the fixed-$W$ all-cutoff bound
$\sup_N\int_0^\infty\|\nabla u_N\|_2^4\,dt<21/2$.
The stated quantitative estimates are certified and are not claimed optimal.
Exact finite certificates and their analytic connection to the full PDE
are specified in the accompanying reproducibility material.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Navier--Stokes equations; critical Fourier norms;
Picard iteration; a posteriori estimates; computer-assisted proof.
