\section{Scope, independent development and research assistance}
\label{disclosure:section}

The results concern the stated initial-data classes. The larger regularity
neighborhoods do not inherit the ordinary Picard convergence conclusion
unless they satisfy its separate hypotheses. The fixed-$W$ constants in
the finite-reference supplement are not asserted uniformly throughout a
perturbation ball. No conclusion is made for arbitrary smooth
three-dimensional data.

\paragraph{Independent development.}
According to the author's account, the results reported here were developed
independently of Silva's work, which became known to the author during the
publication process. The comparison with related work concerns mathematical
overlap and differences; it does not imply that the present proofs were
derived from Silva's work. This records the author's account of independent
development and makes no claim of independently authenticated chronology or
worldwide priority, and no allegation about another author's development
history.

\paragraph{Research assistance.}
The author identifies ChatGPT (OpenAI) as the primary AI research assistant
and Claude (Anthropic) as the secondary assistant during the research.
The assistance included mathematical investigation, development and review
of arguments and code, and manuscript preparation. ChatGPT assisted in
developing the mathematics and certificate programs, consolidating this
manuscript, and orchestrating the verification runs documented in
Appendix~\ref{cert:section}. Claude provided additional mathematical and
code review and reported separate checks. The mathematical work and exact
arithmetic must be assessed from the arguments, programs and certificate data, not
from agreement between AI systems. Neither system is listed as an author
or an external referee. The human author remains responsible for the
claims and for the accuracy of the final disclosure.

\paragraph{Code and evidence.}
The companion \nolinkurl{Code.zip} supplies the proof-supporting material,
certificate programs, required inputs and outputs, reproduction records,
and instructions for inspecting and replaying the reported finite checks.
It supports reproducibility alongside the written analytic proof. This

verification, or independent human peer review.
